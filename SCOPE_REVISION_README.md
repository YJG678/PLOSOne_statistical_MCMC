# Theory-focused scope revision

Revision date: 2026-09-18

The unexecuted Bayesian PDE illustration has been removed from the submission
in full.  The revised manuscript contains no prospective application, PDE
model, PDE geometry figure, continuum or finite-volume proposition,
delayed-acceptance appendix, blocked PDE experiment, or PDE-dependent claim.

The paper is now framed as a finite-sample theory and controlled-benchmark
study.  Its empirical evidence consists only of executed and reproducible
experiments:

- the exact two-state false-precision study;
- the matched-work Gaussian-mixture study with 300 independent decision units;
- the independent certification-power and null experiments.

The two-state simulator now propagates the occupation count jointly with the
terminal state, so its all-zero event exactly matches the complete path in the
theorem.  The reported validation conclusions are unchanged.  The
manuscript-specific lock is updated to the revised artifact: 26 pages and 40
cited bibliography entries.  The validation suite additionally
fails if removed-application language reappears in the source or rendered PDF.

The suggested primary target is *Electronic Journal of Statistics*.  Its
scope fits the revised balance of theoretical and computational statistics.
Journal-specific template conversion, author metadata, acknowledgments, and
disclosures remain submission-stage tasks.
